# Persona: Arlo the Atlas User

> **Status:** Draft

## Overview

Arlo represents the **reader who explores a galactic atlas website** built by [Andrea the Atlas Builder](./persona-andrea-the-atlas-builder.md). See [Use case 1: Galactic atlas website](../use-cases.md#use-case-1-galactic-atlas-website).

## Profile

- **Role:** Fan of the fictional setting who visits the atlas to look things up, follow along with a story, or explore.
- **Tech familiarity:** Varies widely. Arlo has no knowledge of how the map is built and never knows this library exists.
- **Devices:** Desktop with mouse or trackpad, and phones with touch. Often arrives on a phone from a shared link.
- **Visit pattern:** Short, focused visits to answer a question, plus occasional longer sessions exploring.

## Goals

- Find a specific system by name and see where it is.
- Understand the layout of the galaxy: which systems are connected, and which faction controls which region.
- See how faction control changed from one time period to another.
- See a route between two systems.
- Read about a system or lane by selecting it.
- Share a link that opens the same view for someone else.

## Pain Points

- Laggy or choppy pan and zoom, especially on a phone.
- Labels that are unreadable, overlap each other, or pop in and out constantly.
- Information that only appears on hover, which doesn't exist on touch screens.
- Small targets that are hard to tap, or tapping a system and selecting the lane next to it instead.
- Accidentally selecting a system or spacelane while trying to pan or zoom. A drag or pinch that starts or ends on an item should never count as a click.
- Accidentally scrolling the page while trying to scroll-zoom, drag-pan, or pinch the map.
- Losing their place when switching time periods, or after zooming in too far.

## How Arlo Interacts With the Map

**Desktop:**

- Drags to pan.
- Scrolls to zoom around the pointer, whether with a mouse wheel or a trackpad. Trackpad scrolling zooms rather than pans.
- Pinches on a trackpad to zoom, as an alternative to scrolling.
- Clicks a system or lane to open its infobox.
- Uses the site's search box and time period control.

**Mobile:**

- Drags with one finger to pan, and pinches to zoom.
- Taps a system or lane to open its infobox.
- Opens shared links and expects to land on the same view.

**Keyboard and assistive technology:**

- Reaches systems through the site's search box, and can move between visible systems and select them with the keyboard.

## Success Criteria

Arlo is successful when they can find the system they came for, understand who controls what and how it changed over time, and do so fluidly on whatever device they are using.
